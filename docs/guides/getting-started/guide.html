PART 1

Get to the game

  1. Open https://nue-uno-prod.web.app in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari — laptop or phone).
  2. You'll land on the NUE UNO sign-in page. Only @nuesynergy.com emails work — anything else shows "Use your @nuesynergy.com work email."
  3. Tip: on a phone, use your browser's Add to Home Screen to keep Nue Uno one tap away.
PART 2

Create and set up your account

  1. Tap the Create account tab.
  2. Enter your Work email.
  3. Choose a password — 8 or more characters.
  4. Tap Create account. No email verification needed — you're straight in.

Already have an account? Use the Sign in tab. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? or Email me a link instead.

The Create account tab

Then, set up your player card (about 30 seconds)

  • Display name — what other players see (up to 24 characters).
  • Avatar & colour — pick an animal and a colour.
  • Department — choose yours from the list.
  • Will you be at the in-office event? — Yes / Not sure / No. Only in-office players can be seeded into the bracket.
  • Tap Let's play.
New to Uno? A 2-minute tutorial starts next — tap Start the tutorial to play a practice game against Coach, or ← Skip for now. You can also use Practice vs bots any time from the lobby.

Seeing "Almost there" after sign-up? An organizer needs to approve your account — you'll get in automatically once they do.

PART 3

Create a table — table lead

Want to play with specific coworkers? Be the table lead: you open the table, share the link, and start the game.

Lobby — Start a table
Waiting room — share the link, then start
  1. In the lobby, find the Start a table panel.
  2. Pick the seats: 2 seats, 3 seats or 4 seats (4 is the default and the most fun).
  3. Tap Create table. You're taken to the waiting room.
  4. Tap Copy link next to Invite link and send it to your coworkers in Teams or email. Your table also shows up under Open tables for anyone in the lobby.
  5. When enough players have taken a seat, tap Start game. It's available to you, the host, once 2 or more players are seated — until then it says Waiting for players…
Every game counts. During pool play every game scores leaderboard and bracket-seeding points at any table size — even 2 players. So start tables whenever you can; don't wait for a full house.

Changed your mind? Leave table hands hosting to the next seated player. Stuck waiting? Tap ← Lobby and try ⚡ Quick Match (ranked).

PART 4

Join a table and play

Three easy ways to get a seat:

⚡ Quick MatchTap ⚡ Quick Match (ranked) in the lobby. We drop you at an open ranked table, or start a new 4-seat table that begins automatically when it's full. Fastest way to get a game.
Open tablesScroll to Open tables in the lobby, find a table that has room (e.g. "Priya's table · 2/4") and tap Join. It says Full when there's no room.
A link from the leadOpen the invite link you were sent, then tap Take a seat. If it says Table full, head back to the lobby.

Once you're seated, wait in the waiting room — the table lead starts the game (Quick Match tables start by themselves when full). You can only sit at one table at a time; use the "You're seated at a table" banner to jump back.

How a turn works

  • Your turn shows in gold at the top right. You have 30 seconds.
  • Play a card that matches the colour or number of the top card — just tap it.
  • No match? Tap the deck to Draw a card. If the drawn card is playable, play it or tap Pass.
  • UNO! When you're down to one card, tap UNO! first. If another player forgets, tap Catch! 🎯 on them.
  • First to empty their hand wins.
The game screen — your hand is at the bottom

After the game — the Leaderboard

Tap 🏆 Leaderboard in the lobby. Three tabs:

  • All games — every win, at every table size. This is the one to watch for the headphone giveaway.
  • Qualifier — points from every game (best 10 count) that decide the bracket seeding.
  • 🏢 Department Cup — how your department stacks up.
Pool play ends Thursday 10/8 at 1:00 PM Central, when the tournament begins. Play as many games as you can before then — good luck!
